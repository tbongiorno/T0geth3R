# T0geth3R
Our project, T0gether invovles bringing people together no matter where you are, even after university!
Here in college, there's a how streamline of events and organizations that are a buttons press out of reach. But once people move and go out to work, it gets hard figuring out new places, finding friends, and forming groups. 
That's why we created T0geth3r, to bring people together when in a new place. 
We hope our app connects you with a new group of friend, no matter where you are!
